## Descripción

### English (version 2)

Mini systematic review of 10 studies (9 from 2026 and 1 from 2024). Searches: Scopus (21 records), Semantic Scholar (2), ALICIA–CONCYTEC (0) and RENATI–SUNEDU (0). The 23 records were screened on title and metadata against explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria: 10 included, 4 on hold (abstract pending verification) and 9 excluded. Includes an editable PRISMA 2020 flow diagram built on the official template, an access-links section and APA references.

- **Findings:**
  - 8 of 10 studies propose an architectural artefact (6 of them inferred from the title only).
  - 6 of 10 address governance or compliance explicitly (#1, #2, #3, #5, #6, #7); the other 4 focus on security or bias.
  - Only 1 study (#2) reports an identifiable empirical evaluation (4 enterprise projects, no compliance breaches observed, limited generalisation).
  - Enterprise contexts dominate; the explicit regulated domains are healthcare and insurance. Only #2 addresses agentic AI within software engineering itself.
  - None mentions digital government or social security.
  - 8 conference papers and 2 journal articles; Scopus citations range from 0 to 4.
- **Gaps (one per type):**
  - Knowledge: no evidence on AI compliance in development outside private enterprise projects.
  - Methodological: architecture proposals with weak or undeclared empirical validation.
  - Contextual: no studies on the public sector or social security.
  - Theoretical: nothing links AI autonomy levels to architectural conformance criteria.
- **Declared limitations:** two databases plus two Peruvian repositories (no IEEE Xplore, ACM DL or Web of Science); screening on title and metadata by a single reviewer; 7 of 10 included studies read at title and metadata level only; no formal quality appraisal; grey literature excluded; 4 records on hold.
- **Changes from version 1:** from a single database (17 records, first 10 taken) to 23 records screened by criteria; PRISMA numbers updated (23 → 13 excluded → 10 included); new access-links section; unified references; corrected Alenezi et al. quotation; editable PRISMA diagram.
- **Pending:** verify the Stein et al. quotation against the abstract; read the abstracts of the 7 title-only studies and the 4 records on hold; confirm publisher links for #1, #4, #5, #8, #9 and #10; assign the recorded IEEE Xplore links; generate the diagram with the PRISMA web tool if the course requires it.

- **Hallazgos:**
  - 8 de 10 estudios proponen un artefacto arquitectónico (6 de ellos inferidos solo del título).
